Privacy Policy

App Name: All Casual Games
Application Package: com.newgames.all_online_games
Effective Date: August 5, 2026

Welcome to All Casual Games ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your information when you use our Android application.

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access or use the application.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require users to register an account, log in, or provide personal identity details such as names, passwords, or telephone numbers to play games inside All Casual Games. However, to ensure smooth app performance, analytics, and non-intrusive advertising, certain technical data is collected automatically by integrated third-party SDKs.

A. Information Collected Automatically by Third-Party SDKs

  • Device Identifiers: Google Android Advertising ID (AAID), Firebase Instance ID, and FCM Push Notification Tokens.
  • Device Metadata: Device model, manufacturer, operating system version, system language, screen resolution, and network type (Wi-Fi/Cellular).
  • Usage & Analytics Data: In-app screen views, games played, search terms entered in app search, session duration, and app interactions.
  • Crash & Performance Diagnostics: Stack traces, crash logs, and hardware status logs collected by Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose and resolve software bugs.

B. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

  • Favorites & Recent Games: List of games marked as favorite and recently played games are stored locally on your device via Android SharedPreferences. This data is kept strictly on your local device storage and is not transferred to any external server.

C. Voluntary User Support Communications

  • If you send us feedback, bug reports, or feature requests via email, we receive your email address and any details included in your email message. This information is used strictly to respond to your support request.

2. Permissions Requested by the App

Our application requests only the essential Android system permissions necessary for core app functionality and Google Play policy compliance:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to stream and load HTML5 casual games, fetch game thumbnails, and load advertisement units.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required on Android 13 (API 33) and above to send opt-in push notifications about new games, trending titles, and app features. Users can disable notifications at any time in system settings.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: Required on Android 13+ to allow third-party ad networks (AppLovin MAX & Google Play Services) to access the Advertising ID for frequency capping, ad attribution, and non-malicious ad serving.

3. Data We DO NOT Collect

To ensure full privacy protection, All Casual Games explicitly DOES NOT collect or request:

  • No Precise GPS or Real-Time Location Data.
  • No Contact Lists, Address Book, or Phone Records.
  • No Camera, Audio Recordings, or Microphone Access.
  • No Financial Credentials, Credit Cards, or Payment Details.
  • No Biometric, Health, or Fitness Data.

5. Cookies and WebView Storage

All Casual Games utilizes Android WebViews to render HTML5 games directly within the app. HTML5 games may utilize standard Web Storage technologies (such as LocalStorage, SessionStorage, and DOM Storage Cookies) to retain game progress, high scores, and audio settings locally within your WebView container. These cookies and local storage tokens are managed by your device's web browser component and can be cleared at any time via Android App Settings.

6. Third-Party Game Content & External Links

The app offers access to online casual web games hosted by third-party game content providers. While we strive to feature only safe, high-quality games, third-party games played inside the WebView may have their own independent privacy practices. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites or games.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Our app is intended for a general audience (ages 13 and above). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can promptly take appropriate corrective action.

8. Data Security & Retention

We prioritize the security of your data. All communication between the app and external service providers takes place over secure, encrypted HTTPS/SSL protocols. Automatically collected telemetry data is retained by third-party SDK providers (Google Firebase & AppLovin) strictly in accordance with their standard data retention schedules.

9. User Choice, Opt-Out & Data Deletion Rights

You have full control over your privacy and data processing rights:

  • Resetting or Deleting Advertising ID: You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time via your device settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset Advertising ID / Delete Advertising ID.
  • Disabling Push Notifications: You can opt out of push notifications via: Settings > Apps > All Casual Games > Notifications > Turn Off.
  • Clearing Local App Data: You can clear all locally stored favorites and game history by navigating to: Settings > Apps > All Casual Games > Storage > Clear Data.
  • Requesting Data Deletion: If you wish to request the deletion of any communication data or have questions regarding data processing, email us at worldwidecompany13@gmail.com.
